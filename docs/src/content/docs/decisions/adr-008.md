**Date**: 2026-09-29
**Status**: Decided

## Context

Three claims (the install, the sign-in round trip, fail-closed) hold only against the live install and need a credential that must stay in the cluster. A credential typed into a repo-host secret store puts a human gate on the evidence path.

## Decision

A third released image, `rb-workshops-smoke`, carries the deployed Playwright suite and its runtime. The chart renders an ArgoCD PostSync hook Job, gated on `smoke.enabled` and never rendered while paused, with its own ServiceAccount, Role and RoleBinding. At boot the Job reads the realm-admin password the identity grant minted, logs in through the realm's admin-cli client, creates the realm-local test user if absent, mints its password into the app-namespace Secret `rb-workshops-smoke` create-if-absent, runs the suite, and writes ConfigMap `smoke-result` (label `foundry.dev/smoke-result: "true"`, key `result.json`), which the platform's reporter serves for the studio's evidence chain. The backoff limit outlasts the realm grant's convergence.

## Rationale

- The credential never leaves the cluster; only the verdict travels.
- A PostSync hook couples the verdict to the sync, so every promotion carries a fresh one and a paused app fires no Playwright pods.
- Two RBAC rules per resource, because create cannot be scoped by resource name.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

- **A CronJob**: verdicts on a tag other than the promoted one, and Playwright pods at a paused app.
- **A deployed job in the repository's CI with a copied credential**: a human gate on the evidence path, and a trace-leak hazard.
